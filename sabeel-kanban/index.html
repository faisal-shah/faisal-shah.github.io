What’s new in v0.11.2

An alert can be marked unread again. There was no way to say “I have seen this, deal with it later”. Each row now carries one envelope button that shows the state and flips it. Marking something unread notifies nobody — nothing is sent again.

Beside it, a filter that shows only what is unread. It narrows the list itself rather than hiding rows after the fact, so an unread alert sitting behind fifty read ones still appears.

The Owner switch in Board settings says what it is. It was an unlabelled switch beside every name, and the manual cheerfully told you to “turn on Owner” — a label that was not on the screen. Members now opens with a line explaining what the column does.

The @mention list appears at the cursor. It used to sit above the whole comment box, so on a long comment it was far from the “@” you had just typed, and on a phone often off screen. It now behaves like autocomplete in an editor: at the cursor, flipping above when there is no room below.

Three faults found while testing that were fixed with it: the third name in the list could not be tapped, because the section below the comment box was drawn over it; the list flashed up and vanished again after a fifth of a second on Android; and typing “@” then moving the cursor to the next line re-opened the whole list with the cursor nowhere near it.

What’s new in v0.10.2

Notifications ask when you are ready, not the moment you sign in. The phone app used to pop up Android’s “allow notifications?” box while you were still waiting to be approved — before you could see a single board, with nothing yet to be notified about. Android only offers that box twice, ever, so saying no there was easy to do and hard to undo. Now the Boards screen has a card with Enable notifications on it, and nothing asks until you press it.

If you had already allowed notifications, nothing changes and nothing asks again.

The notification screen now keeps up with your phone settings. If it said notifications were blocked and sent you to Open settings, turning them on and coming back left it still saying blocked. It now shows the new state as soon as you return.

What’s new in v0.10.1

Board owners can add people to their board. If you own a board, Board settings → Members now lets you add anyone on the team, not just remove them. This never worked before — owners were told only admins could browse the list — so if you gave up and asked an admin, you no longer need to.

Removing someone is unchanged, and the person who created a board still cannot be taken off it by anyone but an admin.

What’s new in v0.10.0

On a phone, sign in on the website once first. The phone app now signs in existing accounts only — it no longer sets new ones up. So the first time, open the web app and sign in there; after that the phone app works normally on the same account, and you will not have to think about it again.

Install the app before doing that and it says “This account isn’t set up for the app yet.” Nothing is broken — sign in on the website, then reopen the app.

A privacy policy. What the app stores, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to have your account deleted: read it here. It is also linked inside the app, under More.

Nothing else about using Kanban has changed.

Get it from Google Play

Kanban is on Google Play, and that is where the team should install it from — updates then arrive on their own. The download further down this page is a test build, published before a release reaches Play; unless you have been asked to try one, use Play.

Do not install both. Android will not replace an app with a copy signed differently, so a download from this page cannot install over the Play version, or the other way round — it fails with only “App not installed” to explain why. Uninstall the one you have first. Nothing is lost: your boards, cards and comments are stored online, so signing in brings everything back.
