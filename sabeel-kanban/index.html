What’s new in v0.9.0

Each board now has its own owners. Renaming a board, changing its columns, adding and removing people, archiving it, permanently deleting a card, deleting someone else’s comment — all of that belongs to that board’s owners, plus admins. Whoever starts a board owns it, and an owner can hand ownership to anyone else on the board from Board settings. Owning three boards gives you nothing at all on a fourth.

You can now see who runs a board you are on. Open Board members from the board header and you get the list, with owners marked, so you know who to ask to add someone. You do not need to be an owner to look.

The Manager role is now Organizer, and it does one thing: start a new board. It no longer carries the power to open every board in the organisation, edit anyone’s board settings, or delete cards anywhere — that is what board ownership is for now, and organizers get it on the boards they start.

Stats, and renaming or deleting a label, are now admin-only. Anyone can still create a label from a card; renaming or deleting one changes cards on boards you may not be able to open, so it needs an admin. And only an admin sees a board they were not added to.

If a board you expect to run no longer offers you Board settings, you are not an owner of it yet — ask one of its owners, or an admin, and it is one toggle to fix.

Get it from Google Play

Kanban is on Google Play, and that is where the team should install it from — updates then arrive on their own. The download further down this page is a test build, published before a release reaches Play; unless you have been asked to try one, use Play.

Do not install both. Android will not replace an app with a copy signed differently, so a download from this page cannot install over the Play version, or the other way round — it fails with only “App not installed” to explain why. Uninstall the one you have first. Nothing is lost: your boards, cards and comments are stored online, so signing in brings everything back.
