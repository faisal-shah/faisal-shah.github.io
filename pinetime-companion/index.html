PineTime Companion

Manage your PineTime smartwatch over Bluetooth — schedules and reminders, alarms, prayer times, weather, step history, phone-notification and music forwarding, firmware updates, and Apple Find My beacon keys.

Use it in a browser

Open the web app Chrome or Edge on desktop (needs Web Bluetooth). Nothing to install.

The browser version manages watches and beacon keys. Firmware updates, notification & music forwarding, and the Find My map need the Android app below. The web app always tracks the newest published build, including prereleases.

Install on Android

Current build: v0.31.0, to pair with firmware v1.26.0. The download link stays the same when a new build is published, so this page always points at the latest.

Updating a watch? Firmware v1.26.0 fixes the everyday complaint: a phone that cannot find or connect to a watch it has already paired with until the watch is reset. The watch used to remember only one phone, so two phones took turns forgetting each other. It now keeps several, and stays reachable after a disconnect.

Download for Android (~38 MB) The full app, for any 64-bit phone (roughly 2016 onward). Start here.

Android will warn about installing outside the Play Store and ask you to allow it for your browser. That is expected for an app distributed directly like this.

Other Android builds and the desktop apps (Windows, macOS, Linux) are on the releases page.

There is no iPhone version, and the web app needs a Chromium browser (Chrome or Edge) — Safari and Firefox don’t support Web Bluetooth.

Testing builds

Companion v0.33.1 and firmware v2.0.2 are prereleases, published for testing on real watches. They have not passed hardware acceptance. Unless you are helping test, stay on v0.31.0 and v1.26.0 above.

Firmware v2.0.2 resets every pairing, once. It deliberately imports no previous bond format, including v1.26.0’s, so every phone and computer must pair again after the upgrade. Schedules, tasks, alarms, settings and resources are untouched. In exchange the watch remembers five companions instead of the single bond of the 1.x line. Start the update at 40% battery or higher.

Download v0.33.1 for Android (prerelease) Install this before updating a watch to firmware v2.0.2.

Firmware v2.0.2 and its notes are on the InfiniTime releases page. Do not install v2.0.0 or v2.0.1 — both are superseded.
