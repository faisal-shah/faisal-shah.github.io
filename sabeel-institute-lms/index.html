Sabeel Institute LMS

Sabeel Institute’s learning app: attendance, class recordings, assignments and grades. Teachers take the register, share recordings and set and grade work; students catch up on classes they missed and see what is due. Students sign in with the email their teacher registered; staff sign in with their Sabeel Google account and are approved by an administrator.

Not released yet. This is the new home of Sabeel Class Recordings. Keep using that app until the institute announces the day everyone moves; your account and your listening history come with you.

Version 0.1.1, a pre-release, October 2026. New: every screen tidied — lists that read like lists on a phone, a plus to add, a pencil to rename or edit, and less frequent actions in a menu; the player keeps a long title on one line; teachers can attach files to an assignment before saving it, and every file opens in the phone's or browser's own viewer.
