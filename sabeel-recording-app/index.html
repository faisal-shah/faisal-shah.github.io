Sabeel Class Recordings

Audio recordings of Sabeel Institute classes. Students listen and keep up; teachers track who has caught up. Students sign in with the email their teacher registered; staff sign in with their Sabeel Google account and are approved by an administrator.

Use it in a browser

Open the web app Works on any device. Nothing to install.

Install on Android

Current build: v0.3.0, published 12 August 2026. The download link stays the same when a new build is published, so this page is always the latest.

Download for Android (39 MB) For any phone from roughly 2016 onward. Start here. Download the all-devices build (93 MB) Only if the one above won't install — it's larger but runs anywhere.

What's new in v0.3.0

  • Every student has their own page. Tap a name in Students to resend their password link, switch the account on or off, and see which courses they are in — tap a course to jump to that student's progress.
  • The browser's Back button works. Every screen has its own address, so you can bookmark a course or send someone a link to it, and reloading keeps your place.
  • Tidier lists. Archived cohorts and disabled students sit in a closed section at the bottom; tap the heading to open it.
  • A cohort is archived from its own page, the same way a course is, and it says how many courses that will switch off first.
  • Fewer accidents. Removing a student from a course, or removing a recording's audio, now asks first.

Students: almost nothing changes day to day — the listening list, the player and marking complete work as before.
