Sabeel Class Recordings

Audio recordings of Sabeel Institute classes. Students listen and keep up; teachers track who has caught up. Students sign in with the email their teacher registered; staff sign in with their Sabeel Google account and are approved by an administrator.

Use it in a browser

Open the web app Works on any device. Nothing to install.

Install on Android

Current build: v0.6.3, published 11 September 2026. The download link stays the same when a new build is published, so this page is always the latest.

Download for Android (41 MB) For any phone from roughly 2016 onward. Start here. Download the all-devices build (95 MB) Only if the one above won't install — it's larger but runs anywhere.

What's new in v0.6.3

  • Disabling an account takes effect at once — not when the person’s sign-in expires, up to an hour later.
  • Archived courses move out of required listening. A course archived with listening off shows its recordings in a quiet Archived group on a student’s home — never as the next thing to listen to.
  • Listening records need an open grant. A student whose access to a recording has lapsed can no longer add listening or a completion for it.

What's new in v0.6.2

  • A student who joined a class later is no longer marked present on an earlier session when somebody else’s mark is corrected — they show as not marked until you choose.
  • The player follows a moved Listen-by date. Reopening a session no longer leaves an open player saying it closed on the old date.
  • A long recording no longer stops near the end with an error when it is reopened hours after it was first started.
  • Marking complete offline is safer. A mark queued on the phone no longer overwrites a later change made on the web, and un-marking right after marking is no longer lost.
  • Buttons wait for the server. Approving staff, changing roles and the ledger’s override editor can no longer send two conflicting taps.
  • Exports work for every course name — a slash in the name used to make the phone’s share sheet do nothing.
  • Clearer messages when audio fails to load, when an upload is refused, when a ledger is opened before attendance is taken, and when a disabled account signs in.
  • Behind the scenes: a student brought back to a class after a session closed is no longer handed that recording again on a later edit; reminders reach a reopened session’s new last day, stay quiet for archived courses, and are retried when they could not be delivered; two simultaneous taps can no longer create two recordings for one session.

What's new in v0.6.1

  • One enrolment per tap. Tapping an “Add a student” row twice before the list refreshed could enrol the student twice over, and their History then said “Enrolled” twice. The row now locks the moment it is tapped and reads “Adding…” until the roster has them; the server refuses a second enrolment either way; and History shows one.
  • Every student’s History is complete. Students created before v0.6.0 showed “Account created” and no “Enrolled in” line. They do now.
  • Clearer sign-in message when a Google sign-in on Android cannot reach the server.

What's new in v0.6.0

  • A student’s page tells their story. When the account was created, and every enrolment, removal, return, disable and re-enable since — each with who did it and when. A manager sees the changes in the courses they run.
  • The library can be narrowed by cohort and by course. Choosing a cohort narrows which courses are offered; the count line describes what is showing; Clear filters takes it all back off.
  • A cohort can be renamed from its own page.
  • Disabled staff move out of the way into a closed Disabled section, with Re-enable right on the card.
  • Adding a student offers only running courses, not archived ones.

What's new in v0.5.2

  • A clearer answer when an account isn’t set up yet. Signing in with a Google account that has no account here now says “This account isn’t set up for the app yet”, rather than half-signing you in and then turning you away. Staff accounts are still set up on the website.
  • The privacy policy link works. It opens the policy, which is now published alongside a support page.

What's new in v0.5.1

  • Your phone now shows what is playing. While a recording plays, the notification and the lock screen name the session and the class. They showed only play and pause before, so there was no way to tell what you were listening to without opening the app.
  • A class name no longer breaks in the middle of a word. On a phone the class page showed “Hikam Foundat / ions”, because the heading was being squeezed by the buttons beside it.

What's new in v0.5.0

  • A new way around the app. A bar along the bottom on a phone, a rail down the side on a larger screen, and it stays with you on every screen — including the player, so you can check an attendance record without losing your place in a two-hour lecture. Whatever is playing stays in a docked bar you can return to from anywhere.
  • Staff start on Today. A list of what is actually waiting: registers not taken, drafts not yet published, sessions with no audio, and recordings closing within the week. Students start on Listening.
  • A class that was not recorded can be marked as such. It leaves the Today list and stops sending the attendance reminder. The register is untouched, the marks already in it still count, and you can undo it.
  • Your screens say when a teacher has recorded your listening for you — whether they marked it complete or reversed it, and why.
  • The attendance report adds up again. Students who left the class part-way through are listed and marked “no longer enrolled”, because their marks still count in each session’s totals. The exported file carries an Enrolled column.
  • Staff can play recordings from an archived class again. Students still cannot — which is what makes “ask your teacher if you need access again” a question somebody can answer.
  • An already-imported Zoom recording links straight to it.
  • A recording that has closed shows what you listened to correctly, even when the audio never stated its length.

What's new in v0.4.3

  • Listening progress opens for managers again. Anyone who was not an administrator saw the page come up empty — no completion, no override, no listening percentages. It had been that way for every manager, on every recording.
  • Notifications ask once, on your home screen. There is an Enable notifications button there and on the Notifications screen, so you can switch them on whenever you like rather than only when the app happens to ask. If your phone or browser has blocked them, the screen now tells you where to change that.
  • Notifications keep arriving. If you had already allowed them, this device is registered again each time you sign in — previously it could quietly stop receiving them and nothing would say so.
  • Notifications look like they come from this app. They arrive with the app’s own icon, and sit under their own heading in your phone’s notification settings instead of a generic one called “Miscellaneous”.
  • Importing a long class from Zoom no longer gives up part-way. A two-hour recording ran out of time and arrived as a failure. Retrying an import that failed now finishes it, rather than reporting it was already done and leaving a recording with no audio.
  • An error message on one screen no longer follows you to the next.

What's new in v0.4.2

  • A session can be edited after its listen-by date has passed. Correcting the title, the date or the attendance of a session that had already closed was refused, and the only way through was to move the deadline — which quietly reopened the recording to everyone excused. Excusing someone new after the deadline is still refused, since they could never open it.
  • Notifications reach people they were quietly missing. A student who had not yet opened their notification settings could be recorded as already told about a recording nobody had told them about. Re-opening the settings said the device couldn’t receive notifications when it could.
  • Listening progress shows everyone again. A student you excused who was later unenrolled, or whose recording was unpublished, appeared nowhere on the page — they now have their own section. Missed rows read in red rather than amber, and one screen said “Overdue” where the rest say “Missed”.
  • Clearer attendance wording. The attendance screen still said absent students are given the recording. Only excusing someone opens it to them.

What's new in v0.4.1

  • The notification settings are switches. They were a label reading On or Off, which told you the setting without offering a way to change it. Same choices, now they look like something you can tap.

What's new in v0.4.0

  • Recordings now open only to the students you excuse. Being in a class no longer opens its recordings on its own. When staff take attendance, marking someone Excused is what gives them that session’s recording — and it is the same act that makes listening to it required. Present and absent open nothing.
  • Every session has a “Listen by” date, and it closes the recording. After that day a student can no longer play it. It stays on their list marked Missed, with the date it closed, so nothing quietly disappears — and staff can move the date forward to reopen it.
  • Students can see their own attendance. A new My classes screen shows each class they are in, how many meetings they were present, absent and excused for, and every meeting with their own mark. Nobody ever sees anyone else’s.
  • Notifications. Students are told once when a recording opens to them, and once on the morning it closes. Staff are told when a class meeting has passed and its attendance was never taken. Each can be switched off under Notifications.

Students: nothing changes day to day — the listening list, the player and marking complete work exactly as before.
